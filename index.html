Our Mission

Quality veterinary care, and a friendly smile

Westridge Animal Hospital was established in 1984. Our mission is to continue providing the best quality veterinary care for our client base, which we consider our family. With over 40 years of faithful customers, we strive to maintain the health and well-being of your pet with quality service and a friendly smile.

Founder & Veterinarian

Dr. Christopher Brooks, DVM

Dr. Brooks graduated from Texas A&M in 1977, opened the doors to Westridge Animal Hospital on March 10, 1984, and moved the clinic to its current Camp Bowie location in 1987. Nearly four decades later, he still enjoys running a small clinic that lets him build real, personal relationships with multi-generational Fort Worth families — treating the whole patient, never over-treating.

Office Manager

Ashley

Ashley has been part of the Westridge team since February 2007, assisting Dr. Brooks with patients and inventory while keeping the clinic stocked with the most current products available. She has a particular interest in pet nutrition and making sure every visit runs smoothly.
